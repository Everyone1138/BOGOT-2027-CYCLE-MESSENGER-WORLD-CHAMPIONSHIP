GOFUNDME SECTION

What was added:
- A new GoFundMe fundraising section was added between the Video Gallery and Gallery sections.
- A DONATE navigation link was added to the desktop menu, mobile menu, and footer.
- English/Spanish language switching was updated for the new GoFundMe section.

Important:
- The GoFundMe button currently points to https://www.gofundme.com/ as a placeholder.
- In index.html, search for DONATE ON GOFUNDME and replace the href with your real campaign URL.

Optional progress meter:
- The progress meter currently says $0 raised / Goal: $10,000.
- To update it, edit the text in index.html and languageText in script.js.
- To visually change the bar, update .gofundme-progress-fill width in style.css from 0% to your percentage.
